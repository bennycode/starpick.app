Swipe to your best photos

Thousands of photos, and you can never find the good ones? Starpick turns sorting your camera roll into a quick swipe game. Your best shots get stars, the rest goes to the archive, and clearing it out frees up space on your iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store

Give stars

Swipe right for one more star, left for one less. Your 3-star photos land in Legendary.

Play your way through

“This or that”, “Lightning round” and “Best of Series” find the best shot of every burst.

Decks for everything

Start from an album, a month or your own pick. Every deck is sorted on its own.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Your photos never leave your iPhone.
