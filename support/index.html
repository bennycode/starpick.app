Support

Questions, ideas or a bug? Write to support@starpick.app. I read every message.

Common questions

Does Starpick delete my photos?

Only when you empty items from the Archive yourself, and iOS asks you to confirm. Archiving never deletes anything.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Starpick has no server. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I change the language?

Starpick follows your iPhone. To use another language for Starpick only: Settings → Apps → Starpick → Language.

How do I start over?

In Starpick, tap the gear, then "Reset Starpick". Your decks and stars are removed; your photos stay.
